# Aaspaas living spec

## Product
Aaspaas is an educational Indian stock-discovery experience based on “invest in what you know.” Readers submit an everyday observation, receive 3–4 LLM-identified NSE/BSE companies, first compare verified adjusted-price journeys, then compare each company against two direct listed rivals in a sourced peer table, and open menu-driven research details (incl. a multi-year Financials tab). Aaspaas shows NO AI 1–5 scores or rankings anywhere — only AI-written lenses next to sourced numbers. It is explicitly not a trading app, recommendation engine, brokerage, or money manager.

## Data model
- `ideas`: raw observation, anonymous session id, timestamp, surfaced ticker list, and embedded company cards with written lenses (`scorecard[]` = key/label/rationale; legacy stored `score` values are ignored and never returned), `peers` (list of {company_name, ticker, exchange, reason}; `null` = not looked up yet) and optional Yahoo finance facts.
- `fundamentals_cache`: 12-hour cache of Yahoo fundamentals-timeseries (annual/trailing revenue, net income, operating cash flow, operating income/EBIT, total assets, current liabilities) per symbol.
- `gut_checks`: one yes/no answer per displayed company, whether it matched the AI lean, idea/session ids, timestamp.
- `share_events`: share clicks by idea/session and timestamp.
- `sessions`: anonymous session cookie, pageviews, creation and last-seen timestamps.
- `engagement_events`: performance views/periods, price-to-quality continuation, detail-versus-intuition choices, research/question/journal/email/limit/referral events with idea, ticker, and aggregate-safe properties.
- `submission_attempts`: UTC day, session id, and privacy-preserving daily IP fingerprint for the three-per-day limit.
- `finance_cache` / `historical_cache`: 45-minute current facts and six-hour adjusted-price histories (also caches the Nifty 50 `^NSEI` benchmark).
- `events_cache`: 12-hour cache of filtered NSE corporate announcements per symbol.
- `share_events.surface`: `recap` or `chart`; `engagement_events` also records `share_link_opened` (crawler flag), `chart_shared`, `benchmark_toggled`, `chart_event_opened`, `tour_completed/skipped/replayed`.
- `email_interests`: consented update interest linked internally to the anonymous session; email does not unlock a journal.

## Key flows
0b. Results page on phones (≤700px): firm scroll-snap panels (`data-snap-panel` wrappers, `display: contents` on desktop so layout is unchanged): Your clue → All stocks chart → each stock's price card → Peer table → Wrap up. SnapNav renders side progress dots (label flashes on the active dot) and a floating “next” chevron; tall panels scroll inside before snapping. Desktop/tablet keep free scroll. Category cards use an auto-fit grid (2 columns on phones).
0a. Shared links (`/api/share/{id}` → `/?idea={id}&shared=1&landing=friend`) open the Friend Landing: “What would you have noticed?” observation box first (same ObservationForm as the landing page, CTA “Find my companies”), then the friend's read (observation, server-rendered chart image, company cards, “See their full read” → comparison). Events: friend_landing_viewed / friend_landing_submitted / friend_landing_full_read.
0. First visit: a 4-step spotlight tour on the landing page (observation box → example chips → how the read works → disclaimer), shown once (localStorage `aaspaas-tour-done`), skippable, “Replay tour” in the header.
1. Landing → submit observation (maximum three starts per UTC day by session and daily IP fingerprint) → educational waiting view (lesson cards rotate every 7s with motion cross-fade, progress bar, step indicators) → Vertex AI company discovery.
2. Results open with “How these stocks travelled”: a combined overlay chart (all companies rebased to 0% for the shared period, hover/tap tooltip with % and ₹ per stock, legend toggles to hide/show a line), then AI-relevance-ordered performance cards, shared 1Y/3Y/5Y control, per-company ₹ adjusted-price mini charts with high/low labels and start→end share price, total movement, period drawdown, dates, Yahoo status, and honest unavailable states. Price movement is explicitly separated from business quality and never ranked.
2b. The comparison chart has a “Compare with Nifty 50” toggle (off by default; dashed neutral line rebased to 0%), an event-marker lane (◆ results, ● filings/payouts; clustered; tap → popover with date, company, category, NSE summary, original filing link, labelled “context, not a cause”), a marker filter (Key filings = results, board outcomes, press releases, credit ratings, acquisitions/mergers + Yahoo dividends/splits; Results & payouts = results + dividends/splits), and “Share chart”.
2c. Sharing: `GET /api/share/{id}/image.png?period&benchmark&hidden` renders a 1200×630 branded PNG (Pillow, bundled Liberation fonts in backend/assets/fonts). The client prefetches it and opens the native share sheet with the file, or downloads it and copies the link. Shared links point to `GET /api/share/{id}` — an Open Graph page (og:image = the PNG) that redirects people to `/?idea={id}&view=recap&shared=1`. The recap “Share this idea” uses the same image + link.
3. Peer comparison table (components/results/PeerTable.tsx) follows the price section. Peers: the discovery AI call names 2–3 direct listed rivals per company (`lib/peers.py` PEER_INSTRUCTION); each is checked with Yahoo (`get_finance` available + market cap) and kept up to 2; repeats of companies already in the table are dropped; if Yahoo is unreachable peers stay `null` and are retried. `GET /api/ideas/{id}/peers` returns groups and backfills older ideas once (AI, or curated peers for curated-fallback ideas; does not use the daily allowance; adds to `ai_cost_usd`). One combined table: discovered company rows (marigold tint, serif name, tap → details) with their peers indented below (tap name → AI "why it's a rival" line, indigo). 10 sourced columns via `GET /api/companies/{NSE|BSE}/{ticker}/fundamentals` (lib/fundamentals.py): market cap, P/E, ROE, ROCE (calculated = operating income ÷ (total assets − current liabilities), latest annual), operating margin, net profit (latest FY), operating cash flow (latest FY), revenue growth, debt/equity (Yahoo % ÷ 100), 1Y adjusted-price change. No sorting/ranking; fixed teaching prompt; header tap → plain-language explainer; sticky name column + swipe hint (localStorage `aaspaas-peer-swipe-hint`); skeleton cells; “—” for missing; “No clear listed rival found”; “Live data unavailable right now” if Yahoo is down. Events: peer_table_viewed, peer_metric_explained, peer_reason_opened.
3b. Recap shows written lenses (AI-written, not scores) + a compact sourced facts row (market cap, P/E, ROE, D/E) per company.
4. Company details remain menu-driven: standout strengths, risks, business model, Financials (revenue / net income / operating cash flow sparklines over the last 4 fiscal years + TTM, latest ₹ crore value, change over period, tap → year-by-year table, a deterministic net-income-vs-cash teaching line; event financials_row_opened), Business lenses (written notes only), verified stock journey, research/Screener handoff, or optional instinct game in any order; nothing is gated. An answered/skipped game exits to details or all companies.
5. Recap → learning-style snapshot, written lenses + sourced facts side by side, optional sharing, optional update consent, and clearly inactive broker-interest placeholder.
6. `/discoveries` restores this browser's ideas, recap results, and saved field-note tickers.
7. Studio (unlisted route from `VITE_STUDIO_PATH`, lazily loaded; no public link; `/admin` and unknown paths redirect to `/`) → password login → `GET /api/admin/summary?days=7|30|90|0`: K-factor (referred visitors who created an idea ÷ active users), viral reach, share rate per active user, viral funnel (active → sharers → shares → human opens → new referred visitors → explored → re-shared), biggest-leak callout, channel table (WhatsApp/X/copy/share sheet/download), link-preview crawler platforms, viral cycle time, referral chain depth/generations, top spreading ideas, direct-vs-referred daily acquisition, monetisation signals (limit hits, Plus/broker interest, email list, power users, paid-intent audience), power-user table (anonymised), AI unit economics (token-measured Vertex cost per idea/engaged idea/share/referred visitor), product health, top companies.
8. Referral attribution: `GET /api/share-token` gives each session a stable anonymous token (HMAC of session id, stored on `sessions.share_token`). The ShareMenu (chart + recap) offers WhatsApp, X, copy link, and share/save image; every link is `/api/share/{id}?period&ch={channel}&ref={token}`. Opening it logs `share_opens` (crawler previews flagged with platform, self-opens excluded); a first-time human visitor's session gets `acquired_via=share`, `referred_by_token`, `referred_idea_id`, `referral_channel`. `share_events` store `channel` + `ref`. Ideas store `ai_usage` tokens + `ai_cost_usd` (list prices, overridable via `VERTEX_INPUT_USD_PER_M` / `VERTEX_OUTPUT_USD_PER_M`).

## Auth and roles
The public flow is anonymous and uses an httpOnly `aaspaas_session` cookie. The internal Studio dashboard (unlisted path) uses a single `ADMIN_PASSWORD` environment value and an httpOnly admin cookie. There are no public user accounts.

## Integrations
LLM discovery uses Vertex AI Gemini `gemini-2.5-flash` in `us-central1` with server-only service-account credentials and the async streaming API. If Vertex is unavailable, the UI clearly labels a curated qualitative fallback. Financial facts use public Yahoo Finance quote endpoints and are labeled as delayed/incomplete; if Yahoo is unavailable, qualitative output remains visible.
Five-year historical enrichment uses Yahoo Finance daily adjusted close with returned corporate actions, explicit live/cached/stale/unavailable status, and no causal claim. Screener is outbound-only and is never scraped. Market events use NSE's unofficial public announcements JSON (`/api/corporate-announcements`, browser UA + Referer); BSE's API blocks server traffic, so it is not used. If NSE blocks, `events_status` becomes `unavailable` and only Yahoo payouts show.

## Launch hardening (scale & abuse)
- Discovery allowance: 3/day per anonymous session; 30/day per network IP (`IP_DAILY_LIMIT`, generous for mobile CGNAT); global `GLOBAL_DAILY_AI_CAP` (default 1500/day) → HTTP 503 `{code:"busy"}` → “Aaspaas is busy today, come back tomorrow” screen (event `global_cap_hit`, shown in Studio).
- `RateLimitMiddleware` (lib/rate_limit.py, in-process per worker, per IP sliding 60s): image 60/min, writes 240/min, reads 900/min (env `RATE_LIMIT_*_PER_MIN`). Returns 429 + Retry-After.
- Studio login lockout: 5 failures / 15 min per IP (`admin_login_failures`, TTL index) → 429.
- Share images cached in Mongo `share_image_cache` (6h TTL, key idea:period:benchmark:hidden; hidden filtered to the idea's tickers) and rendered with max 2 concurrent Pillow jobs.
- Studio summary cached 60s per range; the refresh button passes `fresh=true`.
- Indexes added for sessions (created/last_seen/share_token/referred_by), share_events (clicked_at/ref), share_opens, gut_checks.answered_at, events_cache, caches.

## Voice input (Emergent-managed speech-to-text)
- Model: OpenAI `whisper-1` via `emergentintegrations.llm.openai.OpenAISpeechToText`, key `EMERGENT_LLM_KEY` (backend/.env). Auto language detection (English/Hindi/Hinglish), text kept as spoken (no translation), prompt hints Indian everyday context.
- UI: “Speak it instead” mic in the shared ObservationForm (landing + friend landing). Recording panel with live level bars, timer, 30s auto-stop, Done/Cancel; transcript is appended into the textarea for editing before submit. Hidden if MediaRecorder/getUserMedia unsupported or the page isn't a secure context.
- API: `GET /api/voice/allowance` → {remaining, daily_limit, max_seconds}; `POST /api/transcribe` multipart (`audio`, `duration_ms`) → {text, language, remaining}. Formats webm/mp4/m4a/mp3/wav/ogg, ≤2 MB, ≤30s (413), unsupported type 415, silence/empty 422 (not counted), provider failure 502.
- Limits: `STT_DAILY_LIMIT`=5 per visitor, `STT_IP_DAILY_LIMIT`=60 per network, `STT_GLOBAL_DAILY_CAP`=3000/day (503). Only successful transcriptions count. Stored in `transcriptions` (day, session, ip hash, language, seconds, bytes, cost_usd at $0.006/min).
- Ideas carry `input_mode` (typed|voice). Studio shows “Ideas started by voice” (with top languages) and “Voice transcription spend”.

## Live streaming voice (Deepgram) + journal
- Live engine: Deepgram Nova-3 streaming (`language=multi`, interim results, smart_format, endpointing 300ms) via a backend WebSocket proxy; `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` stays server-side (backend/.env; empty = Whisper only; `DEEPGRAM_LISTEN_URL` optional override for testing).
- Flow: `GET /api/voice/allowance` → `live: true` when the key is set → `POST /api/voice/session` checks the allowance and issues a single-use pass (60s TTL, `voice_passes`) → browser opens `wss://…/api/voice/stream?token=…`, streams MediaRecorder chunks (250ms), receives `{type:"partial", final, interim}` and, after `{type:"close"}`, `{type:"final", text, remaining}`. Settled words are written into the textarea live; changing words show lighter below the recording pill ("LIVE" tag).
- Fallback: if the pass, socket or upstream fails, the locally recorded clip is uploaded to Whisper (`/api/transcribe`) silently. Silent or failed attempts never count; the 5/day, 30s, per-network and global caps apply to both engines. `transcriptions.engine` = deepgram | whisper; Studio splits voice spend/minutes by engine (Deepgram at `DEEPGRAM_USD_PER_MIN`, default $0.0092).
- Journal (`/discoveries`): entries open the full results view (`/?idea={id}`) with charts and snap panels on phones, show a 5Y chart preview (cached share image), and keep a secondary "Recap" link. "My Discoveries" is visible in phone headers (tour button collapses to an icon, theme label hidden).

## Guided tours (explain → try it)
- `GuidedTour` (components/GuidedTour.tsx): explain cards with spotlight (title 30px / body 16px), then a "try it" task pill pinned to the bottom (ticks per task, skip step, end tour). Tasks complete via a capture-phase click listener on real UI selectors; the current target gets a soft `tour-glow`; the page stays fully usable.
- Landing tour (`aaspaas-tour-done`): 4 cards → task "Tap an example, or tap the mic". Replay: header "Replay tour".
- Results tour (`aaspaas-results-tour-done`, auto-opens ~1.4s after first results view, rendered at the same tree position in the comparison and detail views): 3 cards (all-stocks chart, ₹ price cards, peer table) → tasks: switch period, tap a filing marker, turn on Nifty, swipe the peer table or tap a metric header, open "Explore details" (price card or a company name in the table). Replay: "How this works" button in the results header.
- Events: tour_completed/skipped and results_tour_completed/skipped/replayed with `tasks_done`.

## Studio additions
Product health shows “Price → peer table” (peer_table_viewed + legacy performance_to_quality_continued ÷ performance_section_viewed), “Peer table interactions” (metric explainers + rival reasons) and “Financials tab opened” (detail_section_opened with stage=financials). Gut-check accuracy compares answers with the AI's written lean (`gut_check_lean`), never a score.

## Learning loop (Learn, daily clues, sign-in, practice portfolios, thinking partner)
Education-only stance: no rankings/picks, no buy/sell/hold, no portfolios built for readers, the AI never calls itself a CA/adviser.
- **Learn** (`/learn`, `/learn/:slug`): 18 lessons in 3 modules seeded from `backend/lib/lessons_content.py` into `lessons` / `lesson_modules` on first `GET /api/lessons` (then edited in Studio via `PUT /api/admin/lessons/{slug}`, `PUT /api/admin/modules/{key}`). Lesson = number, slug, module, title, promise, key_idea, paragraphs[], example, checks[], metric (live panel key), duration, youtube_url, reel_url, published, is_tax, last_checked. YouTube thumbnail → player on tap (youtube-nocookie). No video URL = "Video coming soon" (all lessons at launch; paste the ROCE link in Studio). Watched ticks: `POST /api/lessons/{slug}/watched?watched=` stored per session in `lesson_progress`. Lessons 1–10 show the metric live for today's first trending clue's companies + rivals. Tax lessons show the FY 2026-27 general-information note. Share via `/api/share/lesson/{slug}` (OG page, image `/api/cards/lesson/{slug}.png`). `/api/sitemap.xml` lists /learn, lessons, /today and day pages. Page title/meta set client-side.
- **Lesson links**: peer-table explainers (net profit→1, margin→2, OCF→3, ROCE→5, ROE→6, D/E→7, rev growth→10), Financials (lesson 3, year tables → 4), Business lenses (moat→8, management/capital→9, growth→10), portfolio (13, 15, sell toast → 15), waiting screen rotating link (new tab). Map: `frontend/src/lib/learn.ts` LESSON_FOR.
- **Launch videos**: `site_settings{key:"videos", landing, friend}` via `GET /api/site/videos` / `PUT /api/admin/videos`; the "Watch how Aaspaas works" button shows only when a slot is set.
- **Daily clues** (`routers/trending.py`): first `GET /api/trending/today` of an IST day claims a `trending_days` doc (unique date) and generates in the background: Google Trends India RSS (≤10) + ET industry + Mint companies RSS → Gemini picks ≤5 everyday observations (skips politics/crime/deaths/disasters/sports/celebrity/stock tips/IPOs) → each runs `build_idea` (stored as `is_demo:true, kind:"trending"`, session "trending"; never counts against allowances). Failed days retry after 3h; the latest ready day stays up, labelled with its date. `/today`, `/today/:day`, landing strip. Studio: `/api/admin/trending` log (picked/skipped + reason), hide toggle, regenerate, Instagram 1080×1350 PNG `/api/cards/clue/{id}.png`. Set `TRENDING_DISABLED=1` to stop generation.
- **Email sign-in** (`routers/auth.py`, Emergent-managed Resend via `lib/emailer.py` with guardrail gate; sender name `EMAIL_FROM_NAME=Aaspaas`): `POST /api/auth/request {email, updates, location, return_to}` emails a one-tap link (`/api/auth/verify?token=`, 15 min, single use → redirect to return_to?signin=done) and a 6-digit code (`POST /api/auth/verify-code`; 5 wrong tries expire it). Limits 3/hr per email, 10/hr per network. Sign-in sets `aaspaas_account` (90 days, `account_tokens`) and points the browser's `aaspaas_session` at the account's `primary_session_id`, re-tagging this browser's ideas, partner chats and lesson progress — so all existing session-scoped features follow the reader across devices. `GET /api/auth/me`, `POST /api/auth/logout` (fresh anonymous session), `POST /api/auth/updates` (opt-in consent, location learn). Updates consent is a separate unticked box; no newsletter is sent.
- **Practice portfolios** (`routers/portfolio.py`, account required → 401 `sign_in_required`): ≤3 per account (4th → 409 `plus_limit` → Plus waitlist via `/api/product-interest`), ≤15 holdings, ₹1,00,000 pretend cash. Buy at latest adjusted close (fractional units, FIFO lots), required note, optional change-mind + check-back date. View computes value, gain, holdings, sector mix, due check-backs and a series vs a Nifty 50 shadow (same rupees, same days). Sell fraction returns cash and short/long-term gain split (365 days). Rename/reset/delete. Frontend: `AddToPortfolioButton` on price cards, peer rows (rivals too), detail hero; pending company survives the email-link redirect (sessionStorage) via `PendingAdd`.
- **Thinking partner** (`routers/partner.py`, Gemini via `lib/vertex.py`): `POST /api/partner/messages` → four parts (supported/assumptions/biases/questions + evidence_to_change, lesson_slug, advice_redirected); context is only Aaspaas's sourced fundamentals + the idea's business model/risk/rivals. 10 messages/visitor/IST day, `PARTNER_GLOBAL_DAILY_CAP` (2000), 6 turns per conversation. `GET /api/partner/conversations` lists them (shown in My Discoveries). Buttons on detail, holdings, recap.
- **Studio**: `StudioContent` adds learning-loop metrics (`GET /api/admin/learning-summary?days=`), video slots, lessons manager with Instagram image download, trending log.

## Sign-in options & landing top (update)
- Sign-in sheet offers: **Continue with Google** (Emergent-managed; redirect = current origin+path+query, returns with `#session_id=` → `GoogleCallback` in App.tsx → `POST /api/auth/google {session_id, updates}` → backend exchanges with Emergent session-data, then the same account/session merge), **email + password** (`POST /api/auth/register` / `POST /api/auth/login`, scrypt hashes, no verification step by request; 10 failures/hr per email, 30 per network), and **email link/code** (unchanged). Accounts created by password are `email_verified:false`; the first Google/link/code sign-in for that email marks it verified, clears the unverified password and revokes its devices (stops email squatting).
- Header: one uniform pill tab bar on every page (Today · Learn · Discoveries · Practice) with active state + a marigold Sign in / account menu. On ≤860px the tabs take a full-width row under the wordmark.
- Landing: `landing-top` puts a compact swipeable Today clue strip and a "Learn in 6 minutes" lesson-chip row above the observation hero, both above the fold on phones.

## Location ("where did you notice this?")
- Optional `place {area, city, state, source gps|picked|typed}` on `POST /api/ideas` (stored on the idea, returned in IdeaResponse and JournalItem). `PlacePicker` (landing + friend landing) offers "Use my location" (browser GPS → `GET /api/places/reverse?lat&lon`, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, cached in `geo_cache` on a ~1 km grid, 90-day TTL), a city list, or any custom "Area, City"; remembered in localStorage `aaspaas-place`. City aliases normalised in `lib/places.py` (Bangalore→Bengaluru, Gurgaon→Gurugram…).
- Shown: place tag on the results header and in My Discoveries (own data: area + city); daily clues carry a `city` tag when the news/trend item names a city (Gemini pick field).
- Public: `GET /api/places/cities` → "Noticed around India" on /today: per city (last 60 days, non-demo ideas) only when ≥3 distinct sessions; shows people count, top 3 AI theme labels (`observation_theme`) and top tickers — never observation text or areas.
